Weekly Structure (every week)
  • Mon — Upper Body Push (chest, shoulders, triceps)
  • Tue — Treadmill Cardio
  • Wed — REST or light walk
  • Thu — Upper Body Pull (back, biceps, rear delts)
  • Fri — Treadmill Cardio

Phase Goals
  • Phase 1 (Wks 1-3): Re-establish movement patterns, build connective tissue, learn treadmill zones
  • Phase 2 (Wks 4-7): Add volume and intensity, introduce supersets, longer cardio intervals
  • Phase 3 (Wks 8-10): Peak effort, heavier loads, challenging cardio finishes
Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1-3)
Light-to-moderate weight. Focus on form, full range of motion, and waking up the muscles. Keep treadmill at a brisk walk or light jog. Stop well short of failure.

Recovery and Nutrition Basics
  • Aim for 0.7-1g of protein per lb of bodyweight daily (chicken, eggs, Greek yogurt, whey)
  • Sleep 7-9 hours — this is when muscle is actually built
  • Drink water before, during, and after every session
  • Post-workout: protein + carb within 60 min (e.g. shake + banana)
Heart Rate Zones (Treadmill Guide)
  • Zone 2 (warm-up/cooldown): ~100-115 bpm — can hold full conversation
  • Zone 3 (steady cardio): ~115-135 bpm — can speak in short sentences
  • Zone 4 (intervals): ~135-155 bpm — only a few words at a time
  • Estimated max HR at age 49: ~171 bpm — don't push above 90% in early weeks
Joint and Age-Smart Rules
  • Always do 5-10 min warm-up (arm circles, band pulls, light cardio) before lifting
  • Prioritize rotator cuff health — include face pulls and rear delt work every week
  • If joints feel sore (not just sore muscles), take an extra rest day — no exceptions
  • Control the descent on every rep; don't use momentum to cheat
  • Consider fish oil and magnesium supplementation to support joint health and sleep
Progressive Overload Rules
  • Only increase weight when you complete all reps with clean form
  • Add 5 lbs on upper body when ready (2.5 lb increments on isolation work)
  • Track your weights in a notebook or app — you can't improve what you don't track
  • The deload in Week 7 is not optional — it prevents injury and consolidates gains
Before You Start
  • If you haven't had a physical recently, consider checking in with your doctor first
  • Stop any exercise and consult a doctor if you feel chest pain, dizziness, or unusual shortness of breath
  • It's normal to feel sore in weeks 1-2 — it should feel manageable, not debilitating
